# Composed foundation contracts

The blocked-card foundation incorporates upstream main through `2f8312e70`. Published card recipes and
helper names remain canonical. Where older private recipes used a different callback contract,
the distinct contract has its own name: the enchanted-controller upkeep HOST matcher retains the
host subject and frozen upkeep player; the published matcher retains its player subject.

The published counter table retains Conqueror (63), Film (64), Peace (65), and Mire (66). Additional named and
power/toughness counter kinds follow that prefix; Music is 90, with 91 kinds in total. Raw observation
schema 7 rejects earlier schemas with different counter meanings. Encoded schema 4 preserves the
existing tensor dimensions while adding cost-payment and blocking features. Model manifests must be
checked against their actual compatible contract, never merely retagged.

The composed main wire is protocol 230, with computed shape `230:FDFD567263987444`. It joins
the upstream wire with the foundation's payment and targeting additions. In-person wire 6 retains
its independent version and fingerprint.

The native quantity contract follows the bounded 32-bit upstream API: damage and X use `u32`,
permanent counters and power/toughness use `i32`, and life totals and life changes use `i32`.
Wider local intermediates may be used for arithmetic before a bounded native result. Damage-trigger
readers use the explicit `damage_amount` contract and `matched_damage_amount` reader.

The sampler retains physical-card ancestry across repeated mutations and excludes separated
component rows from registered deck roots. Resolution payments retain both upstream waterbend
and snow-source provenance, including hybrid and Phyrexian payment choices.

Multi-blocking uses the upstream `BlockCapacity` fold, sparse observation capacities, and one declaration solver.
Generator-facing allowances join that fold; they do not run a second capacity solver.
